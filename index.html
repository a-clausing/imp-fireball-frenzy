HOW TO PLAY

AIM & FIRE

Touch where you want to shoot, slide to adjust, lift to fire. Slide down below Imp to cancel.

RICOCHET

The guide shows your shot up to the first demon. Judge the ricochet from there! Each hit deals 1 damage.

COLLECT

Hit +1 orbs to add a fireball to your next volley. Your first returning fireball sets where Imp throws from next.

POWER-UPS

Piercing Shot – pass through enemies (next volley).
Inferno Shot – double damage (next volley).

SPECIAL DEMONS

Scorchlings split in two. Infernal Imps explode and can set off chain reactions.

SURVIVE

Destroy several demons in one volley for combo multipliers. Don't let any reach the red line!

SETTINGS

PAUSED

Your run is saved automatically at the start of every turn.
